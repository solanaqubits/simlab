# Evidence boundaries

Formal results apply only to their stated mathematical models. The public Lean
check summary is reported evidence in SimLab, not a new kernel run here. File
hashes bind bytes, not truth, authorship, authentication or hardware permission.
No experimental device calibration, simulation, benchmark or physical composition
is introduced by this registry. Individual contracts do not automatically establish
a working biohybrid/quantum communication or computing stack.

Exact Pauli phases, strict inequalities, saturation endpoints, independence and
static-availability assumptions remain explicit in the individual entries. The
Steane contract does not certify arbitrary noisy quantum recovery. The IVC ledger
is not a cryptographic proof system. The binary purification model is not a full
BBPSSW/Deutsch protocol. The biological models do not establish viability or safety.
The Third Obligation remains OPEN; hardware authorization is unchanged. The
independent whole-stack audit has not been performed by this publication task.
