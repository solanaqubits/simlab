# Prometheus claim coverage

This public supplement distinguishes a checked metadata relationship from the
scientific bridge required to combine results. It contains 14 editorial claims:
8 central boundaries are directly represented by metadata rules, 3 are partly
represented, and 3 have no direct machine group. The last category does not mean
that the topic is absent from source documentation or review prose.

`represented` describes the central boundary only. None of these labels is a
new Lean proof, a claim of complete semantic verification, or evidence of a
working communication system. The public metadata checks operate on selected
exported observations, not the private raw experimental archive.

| ID | Central boundary | Coverage | Remaining limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| NC01 | Different propagation chords | represented | Ideal flight time does not establish a calibrated end-to-end link. |
| NC02 | Exact and rounded channel probabilities | represented | A marginal probability does not establish independence or a temporal noise law. |
| NC03 | Code-specific finite-block certificates | represented | A repetition-code bound does not certify a different Reed-Solomon decoder. |
| NC04 | Decoder implementations and timing populations | represented | Measurements on different codecs, corpora and worker arrangements are distinct observations. |
| NC05 | Delivered-packet latency and loss | partial | No single rule covers every end-to-end component and the treatment of dropped packets. |
| NC06 | Distinct laser-source energy budgets | represented | A source energy balance does not determine facility power for a communication terminal. |
| NC07 | Energy release versus timing calibration | represented | Energy and thermal accounting do not establish a calibrated start-time distribution. |
| NC08 | Spatial uncertainty versus temporal noise | partial | A static spatial ensemble does not specify a temporal packet-noise process. |
| NC09 | Ideal qubits versus physical neutrino states | input_only | No direct rule establishes physical state preparation and measurement as a refinement of the ideal qubit model. |
| NC10 | Attack-specific disturbance versus general security | partial | An intercept-resend calculation does not cover arbitrary attacks or establish composable security. |
| NC11 | Candidate bits versus certified secret keys | represented | Candidate output and missing certification are not a positive or zero certified secret-key rate. |
| NC12 | Global phase with fixed density of states | input_only | No direct rule checks the complete premises of global-phase rate invariance. |
| NC13 | Conditional density-of-states bounds versus QFT | represented | Assumed density-of-states bounds and their sign domains do not derive a weak-interaction mechanism. |
| NC14 | Observed disappearance versus beta lifetime | input_only | No direct rule identifies beta lifetime in the presence of unknown competing losses. |

The [machine-readable coverage](coverage.json) is an explicit editorial contract.
Publication checks compare every coverage label with the reviewed source,
verify the counts, and preserve the negative physical and security status flags.
Passing these checks does not prove that the editorial judgment itself is correct.

The published mathematical source is
[Leanbook at a fixed commit](https://github.com/solanaqubits/leanbook/tree/aa5f03f2e1cf8777a819946bfd6328c3f66c3f7b).
Its existing theorem hypotheses remain in force. This supplement performs no new
Lean build, simulation, decoding benchmark or detector experiment.

The grant proposal, private raw logs and private knowledge graph are not part of
this supplement. No hardware authorization is granted or changed. The third
SimLab-to-Lean obligation remains **OPEN**.

The executable [public metadata registry](../../studies/prometheus_compatibility/README.md)
uses the coarser 8 `existing_rule` / 6 `additional_editorial_boundary` split.
This supplement refines the latter six into three partial and three input-only
boundaries without changing their status.
